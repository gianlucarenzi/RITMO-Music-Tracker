RITMO - example songs and instruments
=====================================

The programs of RITMO (AppImage, .deb, .tar.gz, Windows, macOS) do not carry
these files: they are the "Ritmo-Songs-and-Instruments" download of each
release, and the folder rmt/ of the repository
(https://github.com/gianlucarenzi/RITMO-Music-Tracker).

songs/        modules (.rmt) to open with File > Load (Ctrl+L) and play (F5):
              songs/rmt128 play with the RMT 1.28 drivers, songs/rmt134 need
              RMT 1.34 or newer (see songs/README.md)
instruments/  instruments (.rti) to load with Instrument > Load instrument
              from file... into the instrument being edited

Unpack the archive anywhere, for example in your home or Documents folder:
RITMO remembers the last folder of its file dialogs.

The songs are by their authors, as named in each module and in the folders;
they come with RMT (RASTER Music Tracker) and are shared for listening and
learning.
