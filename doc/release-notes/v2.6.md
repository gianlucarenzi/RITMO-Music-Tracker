# RITMO 2.6 – the XEX with the RMT player, RITMO PLAYER and the fixes of RMT 1.36

RITMO 2.6 exports an Atari executable that holds the RMT player and the module itself, with RITMO PLAYER on the Atari screen, and brings over the fixes of RMT 1.36 by Peter Dell (JAC!): the frequency tables of every driver, the MIDI input, the WAV export with the older drivers, the tuning after a change of driver. The settings that belong to the module (video standard, tracker driver, tuning) have a dialog of their own, and there are AppImages for more Linux architectures.

## What changed

- **XEX with the RMT player.** File > Export As > "XEX Atari executable + RMT player" (script format `xex-rmt`) writes an XEX with the player of the tracker driver (RMT 1.28 Unpatched or Patch16) and the module of the song, not the stream of the POKEY registers: the file is smaller, and a long or stereo song at a high instrument speed fits. On the Atari, RITMO PLAYER shows the name, the author and the date, one volume bar per channel (8 on two POKEYs for a stereo song), the POKEY registers, the song line, the row, the speed and the play time, and keeps up with the frame at instrument speed 4.
- **The fixes of RMT 1.36, thanks to JAC!** Peter Dell's RMT 1.36 found and fixed several bugs that RITMO shared, and they are all here:
  - the ASM export with the notes as frequencies wrote a wrong value for every note, and with the drivers other than Patch16 it had no tables at all;
  - the MIDI input of channel 16 played the wrong pitch, and with the older drivers it overwrote the driver itself;
  - with a driver other than Patch16 the WAV export was silent and damaged the driver;
  - after a change of driver the driver's own tables replaced those of the tuning until a song was opened;
  - a missing driver file was not reported: RITMO went on with another driver, or in silence;
  - the Patch16 player of `asm/` had an empty Distortion 4 page, so a song assembled with it outside the tracker lost those notes;
  - on Windows the errors of a script run were lost in its log file, and a script run could write the settings of the user.
- **Module Properties.** File > Properties... (Alt+Enter) shows what the module holds and sets what belongs to it: the NTSC system speed, the RMT driver version and the tuning (the Tuning... button, enabled for Patch16, the only driver the tuning reaches). These settings left Tools > Options. A song that uses the Autofilter, opened with the RMT 1.28 Unpatched driver at the question RITMO asks, plays with it for itself only.
- **SAP type B** loops for ever, as it was meant to: from the third pass the song played a volume step lower.
- **Sound on Linux.** RITMO is a client of the sound server (PulseAudio, or PipeWire): it follows the output chosen in the desktop, Bluetooth headphones included, and asks for a latency that does not underflow on PipeWire.
- **Help** (F1) opens the user manual of RITMO, Online Help (Shift+F1) the reference of RMT: both pointed to a page that is gone.
- **Scripting.** `RMT_CONFIG_DIR=<folder>` runs RITMO with the `ritmo.ini` and `tuning.ini` of that folder instead of the user's settings, on every system.
- **Qt 6 only**: Qt 5 is no longer supported.

As always, **please tell me how it works for you** by opening an [issue](https://github.com/gianlucarenzi/RITMO-Music-Tracker/issues). The whole list of changes is in the [change history](https://github.com/gianlucarenzi/RITMO-Music-Tracker/blob/master/doc/rmt_changes.md).
