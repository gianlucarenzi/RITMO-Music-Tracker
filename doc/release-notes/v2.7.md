# RITMO 2.7 – the new tuning, the Two-Tone filter, and RMT 1.36 up to the end of October

RITMO 2.7 brings over the work of Peter Dell (JAC!) on RMT 1.36 from mid to end of October: the tuning is set by the A4 frequency and a tonic, with a custom temperament in cents; the Two-Tone filter is heard while the tracker plays and in the WAV; the stripped RMT export keeps the instruments of the sound effects again; and the player sources carry Raster's own comments, in English. Every module saves and exports exactly as before.

## What changed

- **The tuning, set by the A4 frequency.** File > Properties... > Tuning... (with the Patch16 driver):
  - the base tuning is the **A4 Frequency (Hz)** in every key, and the base note is the **Tonic**: RITMO derives the tonic's frequency from A4 through the temperament, so A always sounds at the A4 frequency. With the default tonic A- nothing changes: the tables are byte for byte those of the Patch16 driver;
  - the **custom temperament is entered in cents** (0-1200 above the tonic) instead of fractions, which cannot express every temperament; the cents fields show the temperament chosen;
  - the temperaments have their proper names and are in alphabetical order; seven presets that are no 12-note temperament are gone (a song that used one plays in equal temperament);
  - the PAL and NTSC defaults give the same tables. Your `tuning.ini` is read as it is: its ratios become cents.
- **The Two-Tone filter** (the `$7FE`/`$7FD` command of the envelope, SKCTL `$8B`) is heard while the tracker plays and in the WAV export: the POKEY of RITMO did not emulate it, while every other export carried it (RMT issue #30, Makary's example).
- **Export as Stripped RMT with "SFX Support"** keeps every instrument again: since RMT 1.34 it removed exactly the instruments of the sound effects, and a module of sound effects only came out as an empty header (RMT issue #5).
- **The ASM export** frames the feature definitions with `; --------BEGIN--------` / `; --------END--------`, the comment style of the player sources.
- **The player sources** (`asm/`): Raster's ~800 working comments translated into English, his label names again, and `asm/Unpatched`, his RMT 1.28 player with the comments. The drivers and RITMO PLAYER come out byte for byte the same.
- **Inside**: the module of a song is a container that checks what it reads (as in RMT 1.36); every module of the repository saves and exports byte for byte as in 2.6. A module of format version 0 (RMT's pre-release layout) is no longer read, and an `.rmw` file with a channel count other than 4 or 8 is refused.
- **RMT issue #8** (an unstable speed when it is changed on every line) does not happen in RITMO: a new test plays such songs with the tracker and with the player of the XEX, and every line lasts exactly its speed.

As always, **please tell me how it works for you** by opening an [issue](https://github.com/gianlucarenzi/RITMO-Music-Tracker/issues). The whole list of changes is in the [change history](https://github.com/gianlucarenzi/RITMO-Music-Tracker/blob/master/doc/rmt_changes.md).
